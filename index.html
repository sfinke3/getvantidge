Questions

How much is it?

A monthly price that depends on what you need. We'll send a real number in writing after the first call.

How fast do we get a model?

A first version within a week of getting access to your systems. It gets sharper every month after that.

What do you need from us to start?

Logins to your ledger, bank and billing. Your team connects them from one page.

We have a deal next month. Is that too late?

Probably not. Tell us the date on the call. The tracker goes up first and the model catches up.

Do we still need a lawyer? What about a banker?

You need a lawyer, and they write the merger agreement. A banker is your call. Playground sold without one.

What happens when we hire a CFO?

We work with them. We keep the data clean and the model running. They spend their time on decisions instead of spreadsheets.
