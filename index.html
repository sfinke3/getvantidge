Three things, and they are the same job.

A company that raised on a deck and runs on a bookkeeper needs all three the moment a buyer shows up, the next round starts, or the board asks about runway.

01

The operating model

  • Built bottom-up from what drives the business
  • Every driver carries a benchmark and a source
  • Historicals tied to the books, then the plan
  • Live formulas in Excel, never pasted values
  • A dashboard for the people who will not open Excel
02

Diligence readiness

  • Data room built before the first meeting
  • Q&A tracker with an owner and a reviewer on every request
  • Unit economics and a model the deck agrees with
  • Cap table and contracts in order
  • A KPI dictionary so the definitions are settled up front
03

The raise, or the sale

  • Round sized off the cash plan
  • Priced against real benchmarks
  • What the preference stack does to the founders
  • Board pack and deck generated from the model
  • Answering buyer or investor questions live
