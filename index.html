I sold a startup to a Fortune 500 company with no banker.

Now I do the finance for startups that don't have a finance person yet. The model, the diligence, and the raise or the sale.

439 requests 714 files 27 calls 0 bankers

From the Playground sale. Numbers blacked out.

The model

Built from scratch and tied to the books. Nothing had existed since the Series A docs.

The founders got a dashboard version because nobody wanted to open Excel.

The diligence

The buyer sent 400+ requests in a spreadsheet. I built an app to run them and every answer went back with a file.

Three consulting firms on their side. The three founders and me on ours.

The nightly email

At 9pm the deal team got the Pooh Report. Progress, what was overdue, and a leaderboard.

It also had a happiness meter for the buyer's diligence lead. I turned his LinkedIn photo into twelve faces to match. We showed him after close and he loved it.
