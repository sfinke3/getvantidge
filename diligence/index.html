Raising or selling.

Raising a Series A without a CFO

Series A investors want a model that ties to your historical books and a data room that answers their list fast. We build the model on your actual books first. Then the data room goes up from their request list and every request gets tracked to a posted answer.

Selling the company without a banker

A strategic buyer brings its own accountants and consultants. Without a banker the request list lands on the founders. We take it over. Playground sold to Procare this way with no banker, and the buyer had three consulting firms. We ran 439 requests and 27 diligence calls.
