Security

Connecting your bank, your ledger and your billing system means trusting someone with them. This page sets out how that data is kept.

2
Your data stays yours
3
  • Each client gets its own workspace, its own warehouse dataset, and its own access account. Your queries run as your account and can't reach anyone else's data.
  • The database rules that keep clients apart are tested automatically on every change to the code. A change that breaks them can't ship.
  • Your data is used only to build your reports, model and checks. It is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with another client.
4
Keys and logins
5
  • Keys and logins you enter are stored encrypted in Google Secret Manager. They are never sent back to a browser. The console shows only the last 4 characters.
  • Each key is backed up when it is saved. Every day a check confirms the backup matches, the key still works, and whether it is close to expiring.
  • Bank connections go through Plaid, and QuickBooks through Intuit's own sign-in, so you never hand over a bank password.
  • Disconnecting a system switches its key off straight away.
6
Who can see what
7
  • Access is by invitation only. Everyone has one role (admin, editor, viewer, board, or connector), and the role is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the page.
  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app is required for everyone at a company with a bank or card connection.
  • Vantidge has full access to your workspace, because running the service requires it. Your team page says so, and it lists everyone else who has access, their role, and who invited them.
  • Every change to your users, roles, keys and model is written to your audit log.
8
Where it lives
9
  • Everything runs on Google Cloud and Firebase, in the United States. A policy on the account refuses to create anything outside US locations.
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Client data isn't copied to laptops, email or chat. The one exception is an export you ask for, which is deleted once it's delivered.
10
How changes ship
11
  • Every change is reviewed line by line before it merges, and it runs a secret scan, type checks, tests and the isolation tests first.
  • Production deploys only from reviewed code, after the change has run on a separate development project that uses test credentials.
  • Dependencies are checked weekly. Critical security fixes are applied within 7 days.
  • Failed syncs, numbers that don't tie, and keys that stop working raise an alert the same day.
12
When you leave
13
  • You get an export of your model and data if you want one.
  • Within 30 days of the end of the service, everything is deleted: your users' access, every key and its backup, your warehouse dataset and your workspace. Plaid and QuickBooks connections are revoked at the provider.
  • Backups age out within 14 days after that. You get written confirmation once it's done.
14
Providers
15

Google Cloud and Firebase (hosting, database, warehouse, secrets), Fivetran (QuickBooks copies), Plaid (bank and card connections), Intuit (the QuickBooks app), GitHub (code) and Slack (internal alerts). Each one is checked for a SOC 2 report or equivalent before it's added, and reviewed every year.
